Before the day: the tech check

YouTube cannot bring a remote guest into a stream by itself. So we use VDO.Ninja (vdo.ninja), a free video link that runs in your browser. I email you a personal link. You open it, and I add you to the show. If I ever send you a new link, the old one stops working, so always use the one in my most recent email.

  1. Run a speed test at speedtest.net. Look at the upload number. You want at least 10 Mbps.
  2. Update your browser. In Chrome or Edge, open the menu and look for an update option, then restart the browser if it asks.
  3. Quit any app that might grab your camera: Zoom, Teams, FaceTime and Photo Booth.
  4. Join the tech check at [TECH CHECK DATE AND TIME] using your guest link: [YOUR GUEST LINK]. We will test your picture and sound together. Do the check in the same room, with the same computer, camera and headphones you will use on the day.

If the browser cannot see your camera or mic, check your computer's permissions.

  • Mac: open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera. Turn on your browser. Do the same under Microphone. Then restart the browser.
  • Windows: open Settings > Privacy & security > Camera. Allow camera access, and allow desktop apps and your browser. Do the same under Microphone. Then restart the browser.

Set up your space

  • Put the camera at eye level. Stack some books under a laptop if you need to.
  • Put your light in front of you. A window or lamp behind the camera works well. Do not sit with a window behind you.
  • Choose a plain background.
  • Pick a quiet room and close the door.
  • Plug your laptop into power.
  • Close other tabs and apps.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb. On a Mac, turn on a Focus. On Windows, turn on Do Not Disturb in notifications.
  • Silence your phone.

On the day, step by step

  1. Join 15 minutes before the show starts. Open your guest link: [YOUR GUEST LINK].
  2. When the browser asks, allow your camera and microphone.
  3. Pick the right camera and mic from the lists on the page. If you are unsure, choose the built-in ones, or your external ones if you have them.
  4. Put on your headphones.
  5. Press the join or start button.
  6. Keep the tab open and in front. Do not minimise it or switch to another window.
  7. Wait. I will check your sound and picture, then tell you when you are live.

While you are live

  • Look at the camera when you talk, not at the screen.
  • Wait a beat before you answer. There is a small delay between us.
  • Stay unmuted unless I ask you to mute. I control what viewers hear.
  • If you drop off, reopen the same link. I will add you back. Text me at [HOST CONTACT: phone or text].
  • To get my attention, send me a text message. Do not use the stream chat.

Important: do not watch the stream while you are on air

The YouTube stream runs several seconds behind. Its sound will come out of your speakers and back into your mic as an echo. If you want to see it anyway, mute that tab.

For the same reason, wear headphones. Speakers cause echo for everyone.
