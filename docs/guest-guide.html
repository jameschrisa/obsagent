Before the day: the tech check

YouTube cannot bring a remote guest into a stream by itself. So we use VDO.Ninja (vdo.ninja), a free video link that runs in your browser. You open your personal link, and I add you to the show. We do the tech check about a day ahead, in the same room and with the same computer, camera and headphones you will use on the day.

  1. Run a speed test at speedtest.net. Look at the upload number. You want at least 10 Mbps.
  2. Update your browser, and restart it if it asks.
  3. Quit any app that might grab your camera: Zoom, Teams, FaceTime and Photo Booth.
  4. Open your guest link at [TECH CHECK DATE AND TIME]. We test your picture and sound together.

If the browser cannot see your camera or mic, check your computer's permissions.

  • Mac: open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera and turn on your browser. Do the same under Microphone. Restart the browser.
  • Windows: open Settings > Privacy & security > Camera and allow camera access for your browser. Do the same under Microphone. Restart the browser.

Set up your space

  • Put the camera at eye level. Stack some books under a laptop if you need to.
  • Put your light in front of you, such as a window or lamp behind the camera. Do not sit with a window behind you.
  • Choose a plain background and a quiet room. Close the door.
  • Plug your laptop into power and close other tabs and apps.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb (a Focus on a Mac) and silence your phone.

On the day, step by step

  1. Join 15 minutes before the show starts. Open the link from my most recent email.
  2. When the browser asks, allow your camera and microphone.
  3. Pick the right camera and mic from the lists on the page. If you are unsure, choose the built-in ones, or your external ones if you have them.
  4. Put on your headphones, then press the join or start button.
  5. Keep the tab open and in front. Do not minimise it or switch to another window.
  6. Wait. I will check your sound and picture, then tell you when you are live.

While you are live

  • Look at the camera when you talk, not at the screen.
  • Wait a beat before you answer. There is a small delay between us.
  • Stay unmuted unless I ask you to mute. I control what viewers hear.
  • If you drop off, reopen the same link and press join again. Then text me at [HOST CONTACT: phone or text] so I can add you back.
  • To get my attention, text me. Do not use the stream chat.

Do not watch the YouTube stream while you are on air

The stream runs several seconds behind. Its sound comes out of your speakers and back into your mic as a delayed echo. If you want to see it anyway, mute that tab and wear headphones.

Privacy and recording

The show is broadcast live on YouTube and may stay up as a replay. Anything on camera or said out loud may be public, so check what is visible behind you. Your guest link is personal. Do not share it.

I use an AI producer that transcribes the conversation live on my computer so it can pass me private notes. It never speaks on air. The transcript stays on my computer and is not published. If you would rather not be transcribed, tell me before the show and I will switch it off.
